Internship Tracker

Internship Tracker is a personal, single-user web app for keeping track of internship applications. It holds one person's application pipeline, deadlines and reminders, research contacts, outreach email drafts and essay prompts, and shows essays written in Google Docs alongside the applications they belong to.

It is not a public service and there is no sign-up: only the owner has an account.

Read the privacy policy to see what the app stores and how it uses Google access.
