Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Internship Tracker is a personal app with a single user, its owner. This page explains what it stores and what it does with Google access.

What the app stores

The app stores what its owner enters: internship applications, deadlines, notes, contacts (names, organizations, work emails and public profile links), outreach email drafts, essay prompts and saved job postings. It also stores the owner's login (a username, a hashed password and an encrypted two-factor secret) and an encrypted Google access token. If the owner turns on Gmail sync, it also stores a few details of the emails that match a tracked application or contact (see below). All of this is kept in a database on the owner's own server.

Google permissions and why

  • drive.readonly: to show the text of the owner's essays from Google Docs inside the app. Essays are read when they are opened and are not copied into the database. The app never changes or deletes them.
  • drive.file: to create and update files the app made itself, namely a backup Google Sheet of the tracker and encrypted backup copies of the database in a Drive folder. This permission gives no access to any other Drive file.
  • gmail.readonly (optional, off by default): to notice application updates (confirmations, interview invitations, assessments, offers and rejections) and replies from tracked contacts, and suggest the matching change in the tracker. It is asked for only when the owner presses Connect Gmail in Settings, separately from the Drive permissions.

Gmail sync

  • What is read: for recent mail in the Inbox and Sent folders (not Promotions, Social or chats), the sender, recipients, subject and date headers and the short preview text Gmail itself provides. Message bodies and attachments are never downloaded.
  • What is kept: only for an email that matches a tracked application or contact: its Gmail message and thread IDs, date, sender name and address, subject and preview (each shortened to 300 characters). Mail that matches nothing is not stored; the app keeps only the date up to which it has checked. Decided suggestions are deleted after 90 days.
  • What it is used for: showing the owner suggested updates on the Email updates page, which the owner accepts or dismisses. The app never sends, changes or deletes email.
  • How to turn it off: untick Sync Gmail in Settings to stop reading mail, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions to withdraw the permission entirely.

Google data is used only to provide these features to the owner. It is not used for advertising and is not used to train AI models.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone. There are no ads, analytics or tracking cookies. Cookies are used only to keep you signed in and, briefly, while connecting Google. The dashboard layout is saved in your own browser.

The site owner's own scheduled Claude assistant reads the research queue, outreach profile and contacts through a token the owner can revoke, to draft outreach emails. It never reads Google data, and no email data is ever given to it.

Revoking access

Google access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app then stops reading essays and email and stops updating the backup files.
